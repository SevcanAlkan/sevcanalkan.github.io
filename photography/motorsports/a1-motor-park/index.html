About the location

A circuit built for movement.

A1 Motor Park is a permanent motorsport circuit near Samokov, Bulgaria. Its 4km layout combines 15 corners with a straight of more than 900 metres, creating a varied setting for car and motorcycle track days, racing, and community events.
